runners), results/2018_marathon_results.jpg (6 runners). Category column
combines gender (M/W) with age band, or "Senior"/"Junior" with gender
(e.g. "Senior M", "W45-49").

[duplicate_source_file] results/2018_half_resulls_1 (note the typo) is a
byte-for-byte duplicate of results/2018_half_results_1 - ignored.

[excluded_incomplete] 9 names at the bottom of the half marathon sheet had
only 1-2 race times each (several with explicit 00:00:00 placeholders) and
no position number/series rank - not enough of the series completed to
rank. Excluded from 2018.json: Jerzy Matuszewski, Alan Pilling, Svetlin
Krastev, Sharon Page, Amy Killip, Jennifer Houghton, Adam Fretwell, Eric
Lee, Sarah Bromily.

[truncated_club_names] Some club names are cut off by the source
spreadsheet's column width and are shown here exactly as printed, not
guessed at: "IOMV" (almost certainly IOMVAC, per every other year's data,
but left untouched), "Widnes R", "Runnymed".

[zero_result_kept] Lucy Fern (marathon league) has Heritage Trail printed
as 00:00:00 - no result recorded for that race. Kept as printed; her
series total (08:02:49) is exactly the sum of her other 5 race times, i.e.
the sheet itself treats the 00:00:00 as contributing nothing rather than
as a real (very fast) time.

[suspicious_duplicate_rows] Denise Bridson and Nikki Salmon (half
marathon, positions 42-43) have identical Peel Hill, Ramsey 10k, Foxdale 6,
and Heritage Trail times, and nearly identical Half Marathon times
(02:28:38 vs 02:28:39) - only Killer Mile differs (00:18:07 vs 00:20:02).
Possibly a copy/paste artifact in the original spreadsheet (or they
genuinely ran much of the series together), but both are kept as printed
rather than guessing which is correct.
