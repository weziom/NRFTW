No Rest For the Wicked 2015 - archive import notes

Source: results/2015_half_results.jpg, results/2015_marathon_results.jpg
(full season-tracking spreadsheets, not raw per-race exports) for times.
Neither sheet carried club or gender/age category data on its own.

[backfilled_gender_age_club] Gender, age category, and club were backfilled
by cross-referencing each series entrant's name against the official
raceresult archive at manxtimingsolutions.com (event 150809 - "Microgaming
Isle of Man Marathon & Half Marathon", 09/08/2015), which independently
lists every finisher of the full race day (415 runners) with category and
team. Matched 39/39 series entrants by exact or near-exact name.

[name_spelling_variant] "Vivian Toro" (as printed on the series spreadsheet,
kept as the name here) matches "Viviana Toro" in the official archive -
same bib finish time (01:54:54) confirms it's the same person. Not
"corrected" to the archive's spelling, just backfilled with her
gender/age/club from that match.

[excluded_incomplete] 4 names at the bottom of the half marathon sheet had
only 1-2 individual race times each and no series total printed - not
enough of the series completed to rank. Excluded from 2015.json rather
than shown with a partial/zero total:
  - Geoff Hall (Half Marathon, Peel Hill, Foxdale 6 only)
  - Rosie Whittington (Half Marathon only)
  - Andy Walton (Peel Hill, Ramsey 10k, Foxdale 6 only)
  - Jonny Killey (Killer Mile, Foxdale 6, Heritage Trail only)
